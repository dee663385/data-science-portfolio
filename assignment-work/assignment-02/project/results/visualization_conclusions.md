# Visualization Conclusions

## Main finding

U.S. nonfarm employment fell from its pre-shock peak to a trough in April 2020 and then recovered unevenly. By April 2023, national nonfarm employment was above its pre-shock peak, but recovery differed across industries and states.

## Industry conclusions

- Construction, Professional and business services, and Information recorded the strongest recoveries relative to their pre-shock peaks.
- Mining and logging, Other services, and Government remained the weakest sectors relative to their pre-shock peaks.
- The indexed industry series shows that the national average hides substantial sector-level differences.

## Geographic conclusions

- Idaho, Utah, Texas, Nevada, and Florida recorded the strongest state recoveries.
- Hawaii, Vermont, the District of Columbia, Rhode Island, and New York recorded the weakest state recoveries.
- The choropleth map shows that recovery was geographically uneven.

## Interpretation limits

Payroll employment counts jobs rather than individual workers. The series are seasonally adjusted and subject to revision. The analysis describes recovery patterns and does not identify causal effects of specific policies.
